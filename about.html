Who We Are

Technology Excellence & Innovation

Trolex Products Private Limited is a next-generation manufacturing company delivering integrated engineering and production solutions across precision machining, casting, sheet metal fabrication, structural manufacturing, and industrial finishing. Operating through a multi-facility manufacturing ecosystem, we combine advanced CNC machining capabilities at our Harohalli facility with the established fabrication infrastructure of Arunodaya Industries in Bommasandra.

Together, the organization offers complete in-house manufacturing support from product development and prototyping to high-volume production and finished goods. With expertise in machining, turning, laser cutting, tube fabrication, welding, powder coating, and precision engineering, Trolex serves customers across industrial equipment, automation, aerospace support products, retail systems, and engineered component sectors.

Backed by over a decade of manufacturing experience, modern infrastructure, skilled manpower, and a commitment to quality, Trolex is positioned as a trusted partner for customers seeking reliable, scalable, and cost-effective manufacturing solutions.
