Our Process

Procurement & Manufacturing
Quality Management

Our butterfly lifecycle model illustrates how every component transforms from raw procurement through rigorous quality stages into a final product of excellence.

01

Raw Material Procurement

All raw materials and components are systematically vetted, analyzed for material certification, and labeled before entering the production floor.

02

RFQ Stage

Thorough vetting of every Request for Quotation via detailed 3D CAD Model review and 2D Drawing analysis to ensure full manufacturability and compliance.

03

In-House Quality

Robust quality management practices are implemented throughout production. Every process step is monitored against defined engineering standards and tolerances.

04

Stage Inspections

Rigorous vetting procedures at each production stage ensure dimensional consistency, surface integrity, and accuracy are maintained throughout the manufacturing cycle.

05

Final Product

Components meet all required engineering and dimensional standards. Final inspection and approval ensures every part dispatched fully satisfies customer specifications.
